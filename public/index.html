01 What the community is for

Admissions advice online is either paid, anonymous, or years out of date. AdmitTalk is the place to ask the specific, slightly embarrassing question — and get an answer from someone who's actually been through it this cycle.

Get specific answers

Post your actual essay draft, your actual numbers, your actual dilemma — not a hypothetical.

Compare notes

See how similar students approached the same school, the same scholarship, the same tough call.

Not just students

Parents and counselors post in the same threads as students — no scheduling, no fee, no waitlist of their own.

Support each other

Deferrals, rejections, and the weird guilt of getting in when a friend didn't — it's all fair game here.

03 Why people stick around

Admissions forums have a reputation for either going stale or turning toxic. AdmitTalk is built, and moderated, to avoid both.

One handle, one history
You post under a handle you keep, so advice arrives with a track record behind it instead of vanishing behind a new throwaway name every thread.
Moderated by people
Threads are read by people, not handed to an automated filter. Reports get looked at, and pile-ons get shut down.
No chancing gimmicks
No stat-only "chance me" bots and no paid placement for consultants. Advice has to show its reasoning.
Nothing sold to advertisers
AdmitTalk doesn't sell your activity to test-prep companies or consultants. Ever.

Your next question is one thread away.
